Class Check

Type your character name. Your skills, stigmas, passives, gear lines and stats are read from NCSOFT's character page and set against your class's build on aion2-guides.info (Cleric and Gladiator so far).

    Build data: aion2-guides.info (, Global Season 1, updated ). Character data: NCSOFT's official character lookup, read through the local proxy. Unofficial fan tool, not affiliated with NCSOFT.
